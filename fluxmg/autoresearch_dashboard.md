## Grouping fix (iteration 30)

The first three lines of this page are round-1 numbers from a quiet machine. Iteration 30 ran on a loaded desktop (load about 3), so compare it only with its paired 0.494.

- **Rule:** after the pairwise AMFR grouping, measure the contraction of the block-triangular solve on the system aggregated to 8^3 boxes. Above 0.7, all coupled fields form one group.
- **Chosen on:** 150 development systems (`round2_grouping/probe.log`). SCORE: pairwise 0.680, all joined 0.991, rule 1.141, oracle 1.185. Thresholds 0.6-0.8 give 1.13-1.14.
- **Held-out:** 14 systems fixed before implementation (`eval_grouping.py`): pairwise 12/14, SCORE 0.404; rule 14/14, SCORE 1.088. Generated by our own multigroup generator, as analogues of Yue et al. 2026, not their matrices.
- **Still missed:** a hard region of at most about 1 % of the volume (blob 0.01: 187 its against 14 joined). Those cases stayed at or below BoomerAMG in the development set (0.50-0.98x).
- **Still losing:** easy multigroup 1.28x; that is cost per group and reduction setup, not grouping.
